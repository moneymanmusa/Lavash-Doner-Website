About Us

Real döner. Real flavour.

Lavash Döner was born from a simple craving: authentic Turkish döner, done right, right here in Mississauga. Since opening our doors on Argentia Road, we've been serving the neighbourhood juicy döner sliced to order, flame-grilled Adana and shish kebabs, golden pides and the crispiest lahmacun in town.

We keep it simple and we keep it fresh — fresh meat, bread that comes out of the oven warm, hot sauce made in-house and every dish 100% halal. Portions? Let's just say most of our guests leave with a takeout box.

Stop by for a quick lunch near Argentia & Winston Churchill, gather the family around a Mix Platter, or finish your night with a hot, stretchy künefe. Our team will greet you like family — just ask our Google reviewers.
